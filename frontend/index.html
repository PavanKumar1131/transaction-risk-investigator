PS06 SPECIFICATION

Configured Risk Heuristics Reference

Per hackathon specification, strictly these four deterministic risk rules are evaluated in Python. Gemini never hallucinates what is suspicious.

RULE 1

Unusually Large Transaction

Median-Based Deviation

Calculates the customer's personal debit median across history while filtering regular monthly recurring payments (e.g. rent/EMIs). Identifies unprecedented outlier debits with exact multiplier.

RULE 2

Burst to New / Infrequent Payee

48h Velocity Check

Detects rapid succession of high-value payments to a newly seen payee (0 or ≤1 prior transfers in earlier months) within a narrow 48-hour window.

RULE 3

Odd-Hours Activity

00:00–05:00 Window

Flags high-value debits initiated during off-hours (00:00–05:00) sharply contrasting against the customer's established daytime schedule.

RULE 4

Pattern Break

Channel & Volume Shift

Deterministic statistical check for acute channel pivots (e.g., 98% in-store card usage switching abruptly to high-value netbanking wires) and ticket-size multipliers.
