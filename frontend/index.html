SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE · PS06

Investigator Decision-Support Cockpit

This application reviews a bank customer's multi-month transaction history against four deterministic risk rules, calculates customer-specific baselines, and generates a grounded investigator report.

RULE 1 Median Baseline

Unusually Large Transaction

Calculates the customer's personal median debit across history. Flags non-recurring massive outliers without hardcoded static thresholds.

RULE 2 48h Velocity

Burst to New / Infrequent Payee

Monitors payee novelty. Detects rapid high-value multi-transaction bursts sent to newly seen payees with zero or near-zero prior history.

RULE 3 Temporal Analysis

Odd-Hours Activity

Detects high-value transactions executed during off-hours (00:00–05:00) sharply contrasting against the customer's daytime profile.

RULE 4 Behavioral Shift

Pattern Break

Deterministic statistical check for acute channel pivots (e.g. 98% in-store card to netbanking wires) and sudden ticket-size multipliers.
